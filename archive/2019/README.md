# Original undergraduate paper (2019)

[Read the original paper](Sambahamphe_FDI_Canada_2019.pdf)

*The Effect of Foreign Direct Investment on Investment in Canada* was submitted as an ECON 310-01 term paper in Fall 2019. The PDF is preserved unchanged for reference.

The [current revised paper](../../paper/Sambahamphe_FDI_Canada.pdf) is the main research output. Its data reconstruction, time-series tests, and interpretation differ from the undergraduate submission; the two versions are not directly comparable estimates.

Several original EViews tables report 1981–2016 with 36 observations, although the narrative also refers to 1981–2018. See the [revision notes](../../docs/REVISION_NOTES.md) for details.
